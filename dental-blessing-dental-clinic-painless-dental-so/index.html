What we do

The doctor looks first, explains what he sees, then you decide.

  1. 01

    Fillings and analysis

    Repair for a cavity or a worn tooth, after the doctor has examined it.

  2. 02

    Caps, crowns and bridges

    For a damaged tooth, or to fill the gap where a tooth is missing.

  3. 03

    Zirconia crown

    A tooth-coloured crown option, to be discussed with the doctor.

  4. 04

    Complete dentures

    A full set of replacement teeth.

    Impacted tooth extraction

  6. 06

    Tooth whitening

    To lighten the colour of your teeth.

  7. 07

    Cosmetic dentistry

    Smile makeovers, planned around what you want to change.

  8. 08

    General checkup

    A regular look at teeth and gums, and a chat about anything that worries you.
